Empowering athletes, building champions, and shaping the future of Indian sports through world-class infrastructure, training, and excellence.

Developing world-class cricket talent and strengthening India's dominance in international cricket.

Strengthening India's wrestling legacy by supporting athletes at every level.

Objectives

  1. Promote and broad-base sports in the country.
  2. Identify and nurture sports talent.
  3. Achieve excellence at international competitions.
  4. Manage and develop sports infrastructure and stadiums.

Vision

To lead India's sports development journey and achieve consistent international sporting success.


Mission

To provide world-class infrastructure, training facilities, and coaching for athletes.

Approach & Strategy

  1. International collaboration for sporting excellence.
  2. Structured systems for talent identification.
  3. Focused regional sports development.
  4. Integrated framework for athlete training.
  5. Corporate involvement in sports academies.
  6. Improved coaching and supervision.
  7. Advanced scientific and technical support.
