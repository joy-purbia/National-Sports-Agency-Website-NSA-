INDIAN STADIUMS

Explore India's most iconic cricket stadiums that have hosted legendary matches and unforgettable sporting moments.

Narendra Modi Stadium

The Narendra Modi Stadium in Ahmedabad is the world's largest cricket stadium with a seating capacity of over 132,000 spectators. It hosts international matches, IPL games, and global cricket events.

Wankhede Stadium

Located in Mumbai, Wankhede Stadium is one of India's most historic cricket grounds and hosted the unforgettable 2011 ICC Cricket World Cup Final victory.

Eden Gardens

Established in 1864, Eden Gardens in Kolkata is one of the oldest and most iconic cricket stadiums in the world, known as the "Mecca of Indian Cricket."

Situated in Bengaluru, Chinnaswamy Stadium is famous for its electrifying IPL atmosphere and beautiful location surrounded by Cubbon Park.
